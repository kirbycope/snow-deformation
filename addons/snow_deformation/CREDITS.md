# Credits

## Third-party assets

None. Everything in this addon is original work: the two compute shaders, the surface and overlay
shaders, the GDScript, and the three node icons in `assets/icons/`. The wind ripples, the berm crumbs
and the snow glints are generated in the shader rather than sampled from a texture, so the addon
carries no image, audio or model files at all and adds no attribution requirements to a project that
installs it.
